## How it works

The native `XAxis` selects day labels. The numeric domain starts at zero and ends at 60. `null` creates a gap because `connectNulls` is false; zero remains a measured value. Curve controls choose native linear, monotone or step geometry.

Plain and Paper decorate the maintained line mark. Native tooltip inspection does not imply automatic category paint emphasis.
