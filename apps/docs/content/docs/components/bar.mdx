## How it works

This four-category, positive, scalar dataset explicitly opts into category emphasis through `BarChart`. Hover or keyboard inspection may soften neighboring marks while retaining their geometry and values. The legend controls the one series separately.

The built-in bar path is deliberately conservative: zero or missing values, ranges, nested/function accessors, series-local data, custom peers and unsupported axis arrangements can make the whole plot ineligible. Root policy alone does not opt a BarChart into category emphasis. See the [emphasis guide](/docs/guides/emphasis/).
