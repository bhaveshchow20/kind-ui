## Composition

```text
WaterfallChart
├── WaterfallChart.Background
├── WaterfallChart.Grid
├── WaterfallChart.XAxis
├── WaterfallChart.YAxis
├── WaterfallChart.Connectors
├── WaterfallChart.Series
│   └── WaterfallChart.Labels
├── WaterfallChart.Tooltip
├── WaterfallChart.Legend
└── WaterfallChart.Content
```

`WaterfallChart` holds the rows, the series config and the accessible name, and draws only the parts you add. `WaterfallChart.Series` draws one floating bar per row, and `WaterfallChart.Connectors` draws the dashed lines that join each bar to the next. Add the connectors before the series so the bars sit on top. `WaterfallChart.Content` places your own HTML next to the legend.

## Usage

### Steps

Describe each step as an entry with a unique `id`, a `label` and a `kind`, then pass them through `computeWaterfallData` to get the rows the chart draws:

| `kind` | `value` | Bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `"start"` | The opening balance | From zero to the balance |
| `"delta"` | A signed change | From the previous balance to the new one |
| `"subtotal"` | None | From zero to the running balance |
| `"total"`, `"end"` | A balance you know | From zero to that balance |

`"total"` and `"end"` set the balance to the value you give; the chart never sums the steps into a total for you. Each row keeps its `value`, and adds the `start` and `balance` you can read in a tooltip or color by. Color increases and decreases with `Cell`, as in the example. Duplicate ids, values that aren't finite or `null`, and sums too large to represent throw.

Set `layout="vertical"` for horizontal bars, with the number scale on `WaterfallChart.XAxis` and the row ids on `WaterfallChart.YAxis`. The connectors follow.

Set `material="clay"` or `"glow"` on the series for a finish; see [Materials](../guides/materials.mdx).

### Missing Values

A `null` change leaves its bar out and makes the balance unknown, so the bars after it disappear too, until a `"total"` or `"end"` gives a known balance again. The default tooltip skips steps without a bar; pass your own `content` to describe them. A change of `0` keeps its step with a zero-height bar.

### Connectors

`WaterfallChart.Connectors` takes the same `data` as the chart and joins two bars only when the balance carries over between them. They take Recharts' [ReferenceLine](https://recharts.github.io/en-US/api/ReferenceLine/) props, like `stroke` and `strokeDasharray`. If you hide the series with `hide`, pass the same `hide` to the connectors.

### Motion and Loading

Set `animate` to grow the bars from zero when the chart mounts. When the reader prefers reduced motion, bars appear without growing. See [Motion](../guides/motion.mdx).

Set `loading` while the data is on its way. The chart keeps its size and shows a placeholder, and screen readers hear `loadingLabel` ("Loading chart" by default):

```tsx
<WaterfallChart loading={isPending} loadingLabel="Loading cash flow" {...props}>
```
