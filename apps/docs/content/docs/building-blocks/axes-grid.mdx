Use `.XAxis`, `.YAxis` and `.Grid` to give a cartesian chart its scales and guide lines, and `.AngleAxis`, `.RadiusAxis` and `.Grid` to do the same for radar and radial charts. They're Recharts' axis and grid components with quieter defaults, and take every Recharts prop.

## Usage

### Cartesian Axes

`.XAxis` and `.YAxis` hide the axis and tick lines and draw 12px tick labels in `--muted-foreground`. Pass `axisLine`, `tickLine` or `tick` to bring back or restyle any of them.

Most charts put categories on the X axis with `dataKey`, and numbers on the Y axis. For horizontal bars, swap them: set `layout="vertical"` on the chart, `type="number"` on the X axis and `type="category"` with a `dataKey` on the Y axis, as the example does. Give the Y axis a `width` that fits its longest label.

Use `domain` to fix a numeric range, like `[0, 100]` for scores, and `tickFormatter` to format tick labels:

```tsx
<BarChart.YAxis domain={[0, "auto"]} tickFormatter={(value) => `$${value}k`} />
```

With two scales, give each axis a `yAxisId` and set the same `yAxisId` on its series. [Combo](../components/combo.mdx) charts do this for a bar and a line with different units.

### Grid

`.Grid` draws dashed horizontal lines in `--border`. Set `vertical` to add vertical lines and `horizontal={false}` to drop the horizontal ones; `stroke` and `strokeDasharray` restyle them.

### Reference Lines

Cartesian charts also have `.ReferenceLine`, `.ReferenceArea` and `.ReferenceDot` to mark a target, a range or a single point, and `.Brush` to pick a range of a long series. They're Recharts' components, so their props follow Recharts.

### Scatter Axes

`ScatterChart.XAxis` and `ScatterChart.YAxis` are numeric by default. `ScatterChart.ZAxis` maps a third value to each point's area, not its radius. Set its `range` and `domain` to keep sizes comparable between charts:

```tsx
<ScatterChart.ZAxis dataKey="requests" range={[40, 320]} domain={[0, 1000]} name="Requests" />
```

### Polar Axes

Radar and radial charts use `.AngleAxis` for the categories around the circle, `.RadiusAxis` for the scale out from the center, and `.Grid` for polygon or circular guides. These keep Recharts' own defaults:

```tsx
<RadarChart.AngleAxis dataKey="skill" />
<RadarChart.RadiusAxis domain={[0, 100]} tick={false} axisLine={false} />
<RadarChart.Grid gridType="circle" />
```
